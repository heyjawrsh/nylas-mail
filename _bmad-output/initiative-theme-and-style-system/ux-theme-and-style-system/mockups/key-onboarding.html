Key screen: onboarding looks step

First launch, after account setup. Rendered in Loft (Loft style, Loft theme, light, following the OS). Three look cards in a radio group with Loft selected; the text-size slider and the Increase contrast control apply at once; Continue applies the picked look directly. Try the Increase contrast control: “On” swaps the step to Loft light high contrast.
