Loft dark: 4 candidate palettes

Show screens at
Night SlateOLED VoltEmber CharcoalDusk Float

Pick one dark palette for the Loft theme. Each variation shows the real Loft mail screen (reading only role tokens), chips for every colour role at light / dark standard / dark high contrast, the 8 categorical colours, and measured WCAG ratios. Standard keeps Loft’s soft register (mockup-exact policy, so it may sit below AA like light does). High contrast passes AA for every gated pair: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 icons, focus ring, borders. The toggle above switches every screen between the two.

One structural finding: in dark high contrast, accent-on flips from white to a near-black ink in all four. A single accent-default can’t be both readable as text on a dark panel (needs luminance ≥ ~0.24) and dark enough under white text (needs ≤ ~0.18), so either accent-on flips, or the contract gains a separate accent-text role. Hex values for every role are in the <style> comment, keyed by the DESIGN.md token names.

Reference: Loft light, standard contrast (from loft-global.css)
